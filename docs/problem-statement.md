# Problem Statement: Cold-Chain Vulnerabilities & Maritime Corridors

## Industry Context
Global pharmaceutical and biological logistics operate within ultra-narrow operational tolerances. Critical shipments—such as mRNA vaccines, insulin analogs, and life-saving intravenous fluids—require strict temperature maintenance ($2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$). Any prolonged dwell time at freight gateways directly leads to catastrophic thermal excursion, irreversible spoilage, and multi-million-dollar economic write-offs.

## The Core Problem
Primary freight hubs, particularly major container gateways like Mumbai JNPT, face systemic vulnerabilities:
- **Unplanned Port Disruptions:** Labor strikes, terminal congestion, and access-corridor blockades paralyze incoming and outbound logistics.
- **Cascading Asset Latency:** Disruption at a primary terminal traps prime-mover chassis and refrigerated containers (reefers), starving regional hubs of transport capacity.
- **Blind Disruption Response:** Traditional control towers operate reactively. When disruptions occur, supply chain managers lack automated, multi-objective decision engines to simulate immediate alternate rerouting (e.g., Mundra Gateway) against cold-chain spoilage timelines and cost penalties.

## Project Objective
**ChainGuard AI** is engineered as an autonomous resilience engine that detects real-time port disruptions, simulates cascading blast radii across dependent shipments, models thermal threshold exposures, and executes automated multi-objective rerouting allocations before critical medical cargo is lost.
